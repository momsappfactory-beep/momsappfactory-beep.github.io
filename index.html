🇬🇧 Privacy Policy (English)

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected in the mobile application Ouija Online ("the Application"), developed and published by Mom's App Factory.

1. General Information & Entertainment Disclaimer

Ouija Online is a casual multiplayer entertainment video game ("100% fiction") intended for users aged 13 and older. All responses during a session are generated by another online player or an automated in-game bot spirit.

2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

  • Anonymous Authentication (Firebase Authentication): Upon launch, a random anonymous user ID (UID) is generated solely to manage real-time matchmaking and session state. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account passwords.
  • Ephemeral Gameplay Data (Firebase Realtime Database): Questions typed by the Medium, letters confirmed by the Ghost, and pointer coordinates are transmitted ephemerally in real time and are automatically purged when the session ends. No persistent chat history is stored.
  • Analytics & Crash Reporting (Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics): Anonymous usage events and technical crash logs are collected to improve app stability and performance.
  • Advertising & GDPR Consent (Google AdMob & UMP SDK): The Application displays Banner and Interstitial ads outside active gameplay. AdMob may use the device's Advertising ID. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland can manage or revoke their consent through the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form.

3. Third-Party Services & Security

All data transmitted between the Application and third-party providers is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Privacy and Security

4. User Moderation (UGC), Data Retention & Deletion

  • In-game Reporting: Every live session includes a "Report & leave" button that immediately terminates the match and logs an anonymous moderation report.
  • Data Deletion: Because authentication is 100% anonymous, uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage in Android Settings immediately deletes your anonymous UID. For any privacy inquiry or deletion request, contact momsappfactory@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Ouija Online es un videojuego casual multijugador de entretenimiento y ficción ("100% ficción") diseñado para mayores de 13 años. Todas las respuestas durante una partida provienen de otro jugador en línea o de un bot simulado por el juego.

2. Datos que Recopilamos y Finalidad

  • Autenticación Anónima (Firebase Authentication): Al abrir la Aplicación se asigna un identificador aleatorio anónimo (UID) exclusivamente para gestionar el emparejamiento y la partida en tiempo real. No solicitamos nombre, correo electrónico, teléfono ni registro de cuenta.

AdMob puede utilizar el identificador de publicidad del dispositivo (Advertising ID). Los usuarios del EEE, Reino Unido y Suiza pueden gestionar o revocar su consentimiento mediante el formulario oficial UMP.

3. Moderación (UGC), Retención y Eliminación de Datos

  • Botón «Reportar y salir»: Todas las sesiones incluyen una opción para denunciar comportamientos inadecuados y cerrar la sala al instante.
  • Eliminación de datos: Al utilizar autenticación 100% anónima, desinstalar la Aplicación o borrar sus datos desde los Ajustes de Android elimina de inmediato el UID anónimo asociado a tu dispositivo. Para cualquier solicitud adicional, escríbenos a momsappfactory@gmail.com.
